Inkling

Write, then press Esc to arrange. ⌘/ shows this again.

Writing

  • Just typeStart a thought. Inkling finds free space.
  • EnterNext line in a list. On an empty line, a new thought.
  • TabNext step, with an arrow. Arrows on the empty step point it.
  • -> nameConnect to another jot
  • ? ! ~ firstQuestion, key point, scratched out
  • EscDone writing. The jot stays selected.

Selected

  • ↑↓←→Move between jots. Add ⇧ to select more.
  • ⌥+arrowsMove it. Add ⇧ to bring its whole group.
  • Enter or typeEdit it
  • ? ! ~Mark or unmark every selected jot
  • ⌫Delete. ⇧⌫ removes only its arrows.
  • EscDeselect

Anywhere

  • ⌘⇧SpaceBring Inkling to the front from any app. Press again to go back.
  • ⌘KJump to a jot
  • ⌘= ⌘-Zoom. Scroll, or hold Space and drag, to pan.
  • ClickSelect. Click again to edit.
  • Drag empty spaceSelect several. Circle them, or drag a box.
  • Double-clickWrite right there
  • Click an arrowThen ⌫ to remove it
